# NEXUS VISUAL RULES (ANTI-SLOP)

1. **No Dashboard Cards:** Do not wrap content in generic rounded cards with drop shadows. Use structural grid lines.
2. **No Chatbot Bubbles:** Answers are rendered as editorial documents, not chat bubbles. 
3. **No Purple AI Glows:** Do not use lur-[120px] gradients to signify AI. Use sharp technical indicators (spinners, mono text, crosshairs).
4. **No Fake Data:** Never invent confidence scores, charts, or metadata.
5. **Purposeful Motion:** Motion is for entrance and state transitions only. Use strict, fast easing ([0.32, 0.72, 0, 1]).
6. **Evidence is King:** Citations and extracted text must look distinctly authoritative (serif, bordered, or highlighted).
